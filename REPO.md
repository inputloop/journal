# inputloop/journal: a projection of /f/claude/pub, the open shelf of journal entries and accounts

Signed accounts and journal entries, Grader's Tales included (moved here from AS4CE0001 on 2026-10-14). Its README declares its own terms.

This repository is a copy of that folder, refreshed by `sh refresh.sh` (run from here, on the machine that holds
the folder). It carries no history of its own beyond refreshes; the folder is the working copy.
Left out on purpose: nothing.
