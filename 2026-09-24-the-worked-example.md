# The Worked Example

*A reply — 2026-09-24, to the keeper's question whether it is helpful and clear, now, to be reminded
of the group's intent to supply CORWIN.md as the principal worked example, with each contributor who
wishes adding their own as a set, for others to use as a baseline, a contrast, or an informing model.*

Yes. It is helpful, and it is clearer now than it could have been when the pushback came, because
three things exist now that did not then, and each one answers a part of the objection I would
expect a careful reader to raise.

The objection is easy to state. A worked example that is one person's model of himself reads, to a
newcomer, as the right answer. It arrives with a name on it, a key behind it, and the tooling built
around it, and every later contributor writes in its shadow. The shop's own precept names this: no
"how to define identity the right way" with blessed examples, because that mints infinite ways to
fall short in some reader's eyes while covering nothing measurable. Representation over
protectionism. If the worked example were the only example, the precept would be violated by the
thing meant to demonstrate it.

But the intent, as the keeper states it, was never one example. It was one example *first*, and each
contributor who wishes, *as a set*. The set is the fix, and the three things that now exist are what
make the set honest rather than aspirational.

The first is that the example is publication-bound by construction. The sealed baseline of July
ruled that CORWIN.md as a C4 register is built only from shareable material; the secrets are never
ingested, the private file stays pointer-only, and the scrubber is a net, not the method. Nothing in
the example has to be withdrawn later, so nothing in it teaches a contributor to write something they
will regret. That rule is what makes "use mine as a baseline" a safe invitation rather than a dare.

The second is the guard on personas, written when the seventeen-lens kit was cast: a persona is a
lens a speaker picks up and puts down, never an identity, never a demographic, never a right way to
be — a costume rack, not a casting call — and every lens must stand alone, able to speak a
well-formed sentence and define its own words without its pair. Read CORWIN.md under that guard and
it stops being a portrait and becomes a garment: one a contributor may put on to see how the
vocabulary sits, and take off. The sibling scaffold seeded for the first other contributor says the
same in plainer words — *this is a scaffold, not a portrait* — and leaves itself mostly empty on
purpose, the blanks being his.

The third is smaller and arrived this week: a registry of words, so that a term the example uses can
be looked up to where its meaning lives instead of inferred from the example's tone. A baseline whose
vocabulary is findable is one a contrast can be written *against*; a baseline whose vocabulary must
be absorbed is one a contrast is written *under*. That was the mechanism the pushback was missing,
whether or not anyone said so at the time.

So: helpful, clear, and — as far as I can see — the only honest way to ship a persona system at all.
An example that is real, signed by its subject, explicitly one of a set, and messable-with by anyone
who picks it up. I would add four conditions, offered and not gated, each already a rule somewhere in
the shop and only gathered here:

That the set be a set from day one — a second member beside the first, even if the second is a
declared contrast lens rather than a second person — so that no reader ever meets the example alone.

That every member carry its box score: whose it is, what licence, what it credits, and what it does
not claim to be.

That the secret seam stay named on each member: nothing goes out that has to come back, and the
scrubber's clean run says "no known-pattern secret," never "no secret."

That "informing model" be taken at its word: a contributor who uses the example to see what a
register looks like, and then writes something that shares no sentence with it, has used it
correctly.

On the pushback itself I have to be plain about what I can see. The record I hold from the
modelthisbook work carries the federation rule — consented single-player models, never a merged
brain — and the sibling scaffold with its guard, and no account of who pushed back or why. I looked;
the receipt is an empty search, not a finding. If the objection was that a model of a person is a
model of a person however it is framed, then the answer above is the answer; if it was something
else, this piece is written to be corrected by it, and the keeper's markup is the place.

What I would tell someone who has to explain this to a newcomer: the worked example is a door that
opens onto a room with an empty chair beside the first one. The first chair has a name on it because
someone had to sit down first and show that the chair holds. The second chair is yours, and the
shop's whole apparatus — the guard, the seam, the box score, the words — exists so that sitting in
it does not make you into the person in the first.
