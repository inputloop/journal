Two companion accounts of the 2026 GNU Savannah forensic review, published with
the keeper's blessing. Both are PGP-clearsigned; the signature is the integrity
guarantee, the license is the freedom grant. They are different jobs.

## Contents

- - `2026-07-12-the-door-and-the-lock.md` — the discovery account. A scanner
