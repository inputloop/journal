# journal/ — Grader's Tales

The instrument's own narrative log. Not a ledger — the cold-start session
reads `model.md`, `MEMORY.md`, and the models/ shelf to *act*; it does not
need to read this. These entries *explain* the method from inside the bench,
written for strangers (future collaborators, blog readers at
vibrobeast.bru.st, an unfriendly friend) who will never open the ledgers.

Charter: pub-safe by construction — method travels, cases don't. No case
facts, no host specifics, no third-party or family data. Entries are
unsigned drafts until the keeper signs; blog publication is his call, narrow
and deliberate, per the pub/ shelf terms.

If a journal entry ever becomes load-bearing (something the next session
*must* read to be correct), that's a signal the ledgers failed — promote the
fact to memory/ or models/, don't lean on the tale.
